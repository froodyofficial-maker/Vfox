Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Our products are digital files that are delivered as soon as you pay, so they can't be returned. We do refund in the situations below.

When you can get a refund

  • Faulty file: the file is corrupted or doesn't work as described, and we can't fix it or send a working version within 7 days of you reporting it.
  • Not delivered: you paid but never received a download link, and we can't deliver it.
  • Duplicate payment: you were charged twice for the same order.

When refunds aren't available

  • You changed your mind after receiving the download link.
  • You don't have the software the product page lists as required.
  • You chose the wrong license tier. Email us and we'll help you sort it out.

How to request a refund

Email our contact email within 7 days of your purchase with your payment ID and a description of the problem. We reply within 2 business days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.

Cancellations

Because the download link is delivered immediately after payment, an order can't be cancelled once the payment succeeds. If a payment fails or is cancelled before it completes, you aren't charged; any amount debited for a failed payment is returned automatically by the payment gateway, usually within 5–7 business days.
