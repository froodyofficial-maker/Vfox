Contact

What do I get when I buy an effect?

The After Effects project file (.aep). Open it, move the effect where you want it in your shot, adjust the controls and render.

Do I need any plugins?

No. Proton Beam is built only with effects that come with After Effects. If a future product needs a plugin, its tags will say so.

How do I position the Proton Beam?

Move the two 3D points, Wand_Tip_3D (where the beam starts) and Beam_Target_3D (where it hits). Use the BEAM_CTRL layer to set when it fires and stops, its intensity and how chaotic the arcs are.

Why is there a logo on the preview videos?

The previews are watermarked so they can't be used as free stock footage. The project file you buy has no watermark.

Which license do I need?

Standard if you earn under ₹50 lakh a month, Pro if you earn more. Both allow the same uses. See Licenses for details.

How do I get my files after purchase?

You get a download link right after checkout.

Can I get a refund?

Because files are digital, sales are final once downloaded. If a file is broken or doesn't match its description, email us and we'll fix it or refund you.
