Licensing

Two licenses, plain terms

Your license depends on how much you earn. Pick it in your cart; it covers every item in that order.

Standard

For individuals & businesses earning under ₹50 lakh a month
  • Use the effect in your own videos and client work
  • Post on YouTube, Instagram, reels and other platforms, monetised or not
  • The price shown first on every product (Proton Beam: ₹239)

Pro

For individuals & businesses earning over ₹50 lakh a month
  • The same rights as Standard
  • Required once your monthly earnings pass ₹50 lakh
  • The higher price on every product (Proton Beam: ₹399)
